# Target Users & Usage Scenarios

## Target Users

Kaayup is designed for people who want to discover music from artists featured in festival lineups without having to search for each artist individually.

### Prospective Festival Attendees

People considering attending a music festival who want to explore the announced artists before deciding whether to attend.

**Needs:**
- Discover music from unfamiliar artists.
- Explore the lineup without performing repetitive searches.
- Identify artists whose music matches their interests.

### Ticket Holders

People who have already purchased tickets for a music festival and want to discover unfamiliar artists before the event.

**Needs:**
- Become familiar with artists featured in the lineup.
- Discover songs and artists they might enjoy.
- Identify potential performances of interest.

## Usage Scenarios

The following scenarios describe situations in which target users may benefit from Kaayup. They represent expected usage rather than validated user behavior.

### Scenario: Exploring a Festival Before Purchasing Tickets

**User:** Prospective festival attendee.

**Context:** A person discovers an upcoming festival and finds its lineup poster. Although they recognize some artists, many names are unfamiliar.

**Current difficulty:** Exploring the lineup requires searching for each unfamiliar artist individually on music platforms.

**Expected interaction:** The user uploads the lineup image to Kaayup, reviews the identified artists, and explores their available songs and music platform links.

**Expected outcome:** The user discovers artists of interest and gains a better understanding of the festival's musical offerings.

### Scenario: Discovering Artists After Purchasing Tickets

**User:** Ticket holder.

**Context:** A person has purchased tickets for an upcoming festival and wants to discover music from artists they do not already know.

**Current difficulty:** The lineup contains numerous artists, making manual exploration time-consuming and repetitive.

**Expected interaction:** The user uploads the festival lineup image, reviews the identified artists, and explores representative songs.

**Expected outcome:** The user discovers new music and identifies artists they may be interested in seeing at the festival.

### Scenario: Exploring an Extensive Festival Lineup

**User:** Prospective festival attendee or ticket holder.

**Context:** A festival poster contains many artists, including several whose names are unfamiliar to the user.

**Current difficulty:** Manually searching for every artist requires considerable effort, increasing the likelihood that the user will abandon the discovery process.

**Expected interaction:** The user uploads the poster and explores the identified artists through a consolidated interface. When artist names are incorrectly recognized or ambiguous, the user can review and correct them.

**Expected outcome:** The user can explore more artists with less repetitive effort, while retaining control over uncertain recognition results.

## Assumptions and Validation

The target users and scenarios are based on the project's initial problem definition.

The following assumptions have not yet been validated through user research:

- Potential attendees are willing to explore unfamiliar artists before purchasing tickets.
- Ticket holders are interested in discovering additional artists before attending.
- Reducing repetitive searches improves the music discovery experience.
- Uploading a lineup image is a convenient starting point for music exploration.

These assumptions should be reviewed as user feedback becomes available.
