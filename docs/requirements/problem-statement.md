# Problem Statement

## Context

Music festivals bring together numerous artists, both established and emerging, offering attendees opportunities to discover new music.

However, festival lineups often feature a large number of artists, many of whom may be unfamiliar to potential attendees.

People interested in attending a festival, particularly those who have already purchased tickets, may want to explore the announced artists before the event.

Currently, this process often involves searching for each artist individually on music platforms such as Spotify or YouTube.

## Problem

Discovering music from artists featured in a festival lineup can be a time-consuming, repetitive, and manual process.

Users must identify artist names, search for them individually on music platforms, and explore their songs to determine which artists interest them.

When a lineup contains numerous unfamiliar artists, this process becomes increasingly demanding and may discourage users from continuing their exploration.

## Target Users

The problem primarily affects:

- People considering attending music festivals who want to become familiar with the announced artists.
- People who have already purchased festival tickets and want to discover new music before attending.

## Problem Impact

The difficulties associated with manually exploring festival lineups may result in:

- Significant time and effort spent searching for individual artists.
- Fatigue or loss of interest during the discovery process.
- Abandonment of music exploration before discovering potentially interesting artists.
- A tendency to listen only to previously known artists.
- Missed opportunities to discover new music and performers.

## Proposed Solution

Kaayup aims to simplify music discovery using images of festival lineups.

The application will identify artists featured in a lineup and provide relevant music information, allowing users to explore their songs without having to search for each artist individually across different platforms.

The product will prioritize a simple, fast, and accessible experience on smartphones and tablets.

## Product Objective

To facilitate the discovery of music from artists featured in festival lineups by reducing the time and effort required to explore their songs.

As a secondary benefit, Kaayup aims to help users identify artists they may be interested in seeing at a festival.

## Scope and Boundaries

Kaayup will primarily focus on simplifying the exploration of music from artists featured in festival lineups.

The product is not intended to replace music streaming platforms or provide a complete music playback service.

Festival scheduling, stage planning, and navigation within festival venues are not part of the product's primary objective.

Specific functional scope will be documented separately in the system requirements.
